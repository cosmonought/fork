# PullQuote

A phrase lifted out of the text, in the volume's colour between two `ink` rules.

## Use
- Italic `quote`, `issue`, between 1px `ink` rules. One per page at most, and only words that are in the text (it repeats them or, as on the call, continues the sentence).
- Inside a reading column it takes the column's width.

## Markup
```html
<blockquote class="fk-pullquote"><p>from memoir to manifesto, from whitepaper to speculation, from PFPs to institutional critique.</p></blockquote>
```

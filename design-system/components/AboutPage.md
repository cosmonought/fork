# AboutPage

About the journal: what Fork is, then its name, argument, method and institution in four numbered parts.

## Order
- SiteHeader (About current); kicker and title; the lede and two paragraphs in a split.
- The ForkDivider before "The Name", whose first sentence is the divider's meaning.
- The four parts as wide ScopeAreas (`fk-areas--wide`), each with its two paragraphs.
- Buttons: Journal scope, Call for papers, Submit. SiteFooter.

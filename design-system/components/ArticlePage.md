# ArticlePage

A published piece: its head, the abstract, the text in numbered sections, figures, notes and references. A template; the preview sets Fork's own About text as stand-in copy, and everything in brackets is the piece's own.

## Order
- SiteHeader; ArticleHead (kicker with volume and type, title, byline, Facts: Published, Peer review, Licence, Cite as).
- OnThisPage beside the Prose: the Abstract, the numbered sections, Figures, the ForkDivider's end mark, Notes, References. SiteFooter.

## Use
- Every piece type uses it: a review essay, an intervention, a memoir or a manifesto changes only its kicker; artwork leads with its figures and may leave out the abstract; a translation names its translator in the byline.

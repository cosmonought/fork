# IssuePage

A published volume: its title, editor, cover and contents. A template for Vol. 0's publication; everything in brackets is the volume's own.

## Order
- SiteHeader; the hero with the volume's kicker, title (`display-xl`), "Edited by", Editor's statement / Contents, and its IssueCover (not linked: you are on it).
- Facts: Published, Contributions, Peer review, Licence.
- Contents, every entry linking to its piece. SiteFooter.

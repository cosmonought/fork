# SubmitPage

How to take part: the deadlines, the form to register interest, the editorial principles, and what the journal publishes.

## Order
- Compact SiteHeader (Submit current); kicker and title; Facts (ruled): the two deadlines and the contact.
- Register interest (a split): the invitation, then the form (Name, Email, Interest, Research area), which opens the visitor's email app with the message written (`data-fk-form`, `data-fk-mailto`) and then shows its thank-you note.
- Principles.
- What we publish: article types, languages, citation style, as Facts in one column; the link to the full call. SiteFooter.

# SubmitPage

How to take part: the deadlines, the form to register interest, the editorial principles, and what the journal publishes.

## Order
- SiteHeader (Submit current); kicker and title; Facts (ruled): the two deadlines and the contact.
- What we publish: article types, languages, citation style, as Facts in one column; the link to the full call.
- Register interest (a split): the invitation, then the form (Name, Email, Interest, Research area). On fork.netadao.org it posts the registration to the Academy's database, where it appears in the Academy Admin under Fork, and then shows its thank-you note; if it can't, it stays filled in and says so (see Field). In these previews it only shows the note.
- Principles, then the SiteFooter.

# Principles

The journal's commitments: one italic line and the four rules beneath it.

## Use
- "Open. Rigorous. Public." in italic `statement`; the four commitments in `body-s` between `rule` hairlines.
- The words are fixed (they are the journal's editorial principles); it closes the homepage and opens the editorial section of Submit.

## Markup
```html
<section class="fk-principles" aria-labelledby="principles">
  <h2 class="fk-principles__line" id="principles">Open. Rigorous. Public.</h2>
  <ul class="fk-principles__list">
    <li>Every article freely available, forever.</li>
    <li>No article processing fees.</li>
    <li>Double-blind peer review.</li>
    <li>Editorial decisions independent of Neta DAO governance.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
```
